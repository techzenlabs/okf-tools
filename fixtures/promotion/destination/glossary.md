# Glossary

**Batch.** A run of work started on a schedule rather than by a person.

**REG-014.** A register entry this bundle carries, so a promoted page citing
it cites something the reader can open. An identifier the destination spells
out is a reference; one it does not is a gesture at a register the reader
cannot reach, and the number says how large that register is.
